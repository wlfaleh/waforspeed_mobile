# Tips shown while map packs install (WFS-306 RG-04, English: WFS-507): one per line, changed every 7 s. Same order as astuces-paquets.txt.
Brush past traffic without touching it: every near miss pushes your combo up.
Nitro refills as you drive and with every overtake.
Hitting the scenery breaks your combo: keep your line clean.
In Settings, "Steering assist" can be turned on or off.
Pick "Arrows" in Settings if you prefer buttons to steer.
The gyroscope is set in Settings: tilt the phone to steer.
Brake before the turn, not in it: you come out faster.
When stopped, hold the brake to reverse.
Every checkpoint you pass gives you time back: aim for the next one!
Cockpit view shows the inside of every car.
In the Showroom, each car keeps the color you pick for it.
The 5-Car Race can be set by distance and difficulty.
In Pursuit, shake off the police without letting them stick to you too long.
Online duel is played against a friend: send them your code.
At night, the wet road reflects the streetlights: take care in the turns.
"Low" graphics make the game lighter on small phones.
Once installed, the map stays on your phone: no Internet needed to play.
