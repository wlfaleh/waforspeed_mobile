## In-app purchases

- The game sells **stars** (the game's currency) in packs, and the **"no ads"** option. Stars can only be used in the game: they have no cash value and cannot be resold or exchanged.
- Everything you can buy with stars can also be earned by playing. There are no paid random loot boxes.
- Payment goes through **Google Play**, with your Google account. The game never sees your bank card.
- **Prices are shown including all taxes**, in your currency, by Google Play, before you confirm the purchase.
- Stars and the "no ads" option are delivered right after payment. By confirming the purchase, you ask for this immediate delivery and you **waive your 14-day right of withdrawal** (French Consumer Code, article L221-28, 13°).
- **Refunds**: you can ask Google Play for a refund, under its rules. If the game does not deliver what you paid for, write to the contact above. Your consumer rights (legal guarantee of conformity) remain in full.
- The "no ads" option is linked to your Google account: "Restore my purchases" brings it back after a reinstall. Stars already spent or kept on the phone are lost if you uninstall the game.
- **Minors**: if you are under 18, ask a parent before any purchase. Parents can block purchases with Google Play parental controls.

## Ad videos

- At the end of a race, you can choose to watch an **ad video** to double the stars you earned, 5 times a day at most. It is never required, and there are never ads during the race.
- Ads are provided by Google (AdMob). At first launch, in Europe, the game asks for your consent to personalised ads; you can decline and change your mind in the Settings. Details are in the privacy policy.
- With the "no ads" option, the game no longer offers videos: the double stars are given directly.

## Map downloads

- The game downloads its **map packs** from the project's GitHub Releases. The address is set in the game's configuration file (configgame.json); the game downloads from no other site.
- The **size** of a pack is shown before the download. **Wi-Fi is recommended**: on mobile data, nothing is downloaded until you tap "Install".
- A downloaded file is checked (checksum) before it is used. It contains only game data: it is never executed.
- **Without Internet**, the game stays playable: the backup map shipped with the game is used.
- As with any web page, **GitHub (the host) receives the phone's IP address** with each download.

## Online duel

- In the duel, the other players in the lobby see your **nickname**. Choose a decent nickname: no insults, no hate speech, no name of another person or of a brand.
- Breaking these rules can get you removed from a lobby or from the online duel.

## Data kept on the phone

- The save file (settings, records, chosen car, stars, proof of your purchases, version of the accepted terms) and the map packs stay in the **app's private folder**.
- Everything is deleted when you uninstall the game. Map packs will also be able to be deleted one by one from the track selection.

## Driving and safety

- WaForSpeed is a game: never copy its driving on a real road. Follow the traffic laws.
- Do not play while driving, while walking in the street or in any situation that needs your attention.
- Take regular breaks. If you feel unwell, or get headaches or dizziness, stop playing.
